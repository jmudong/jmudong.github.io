---
title: "Comparing Forecasting Models for Univariate Time-Series Analysis"
excerpt: "Empirical comparison study evaluating the performance of traditional statistical forecasting models as well as deep learning architectures designed for time-series forecasting across different forecasting scenarios."
collection: portfolio
---

Modern deep learning architectures have become powerful tools for prediction and time-series forecasting, being able to reach high accuracy metrics
for high dimensional, large sample datasets. However, they are not without their limitations, as they are particularly prone to overfitting for
relatively smaller sample sizes. In this case, we turn to traditional time-series forecasting methods, and find that they perform better than
deep learning architectures for "small" sample size forecasting. 

This work was done under the UW course CSE 599G1: Deep Learning for Computer Vision, taught in Spring 2026.
