I previously earned my B.S. in Statistics at the University of California, Davis, with a concentration in Statistical Data Science and a minor in Geographic Information Systems. 

My interests mostly spans across the broader data analytics and data science field. Recently, I've focused on integrating classical statistics with modern AI/ML models, especially in regards to benchmarking and validation. My work has been applied through a variety of domains, including oceanography, supply chain management, education, atmospheric sciences, materials sciences, and much more.
